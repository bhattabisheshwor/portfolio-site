Product designer. I design interfaces that feel obvious to use and brands that feel alive, then build them so what ships matches the design.

My work covers research and design of Document based Fraud detection platform used by globally recognized Insurances and banks. Along with ed-tech platforms used around the world, e-commerce, construction and university apps. On the brand side I design logos, mascots, merch and illustration. I like to closely work with motion in UI, which means the motion, spacing and details you see in the design are the ones that go live.
