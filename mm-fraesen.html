Overview

MM Fräsen provides Robotic CNC Milling Service for Wood and Plastic. It uses 7-axis precision milling robot to supply one-offs and small batches of products.

02

Problem

MM Fräsen was using a very generic logo using only its name. It didn't have a proper brand mark, let alone a visual identity that truly addressesd the service MM Fräsen has been providing. A designer who was working on the project previously already had worked on new color palette, brand mark and typography but they were not satisfied with the result.

We want new brand mark to address our premium products as well as batch-offs.
03

Solution

The client already liked the color palette other designer worked on. But, they were not satisfied with the brand marks and typography. That is when they contacted me to work on the project.
