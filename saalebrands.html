02

Problem

Being busy on helping other businesses, Saalebrands itself hadn't had the time to focus on its own brand identity and have been using the logo they first used when establishing the business. The logo was not effective to showcase what the brand actually is and what is does. So, it needed a fresh new look.

How can we establish a string presence as a leading branding agency in the region?
03

Solution

Saalebrands already had a very strong brand name, named after the Saale River, which flows through the region and is an important part of the local identity. When brain stroming the idea together, we decided to use the river as a integral part of the new brand identity. I, then worked with the Saale meanders, inside a square shape. Which now looks like two puzzle pieces perfect fit with each other. Same as the Saalebrands and its clients.
