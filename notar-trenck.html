02

Problem

The notary was using the State coat of arms and their family coat of arms as a logo.

Is there a way to connect my family coat of arms to modern branding?
03

Solution

The family coat of arms was very beautiful, with meaningful elements associated with their family. But, it's too cluttered to be a business logo. I experimented a bit and used coat of shield, letter T and roman pillar to associate the legal profession. I simplified the color from the coat of arms and paired the brand mark with a think Serif font to give it a premium looks. I also developed brand guidelines and 2D animation of th elogo for better brand consistency and visibility.
