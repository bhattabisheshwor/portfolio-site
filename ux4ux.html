02

Problem

Usability is an element that affects the efficiency and use of medical research software, but many researchers in the medical sector are unaware of appropriate UX processes and usability testing best practices in their fields. Therefore, many non UX experts fail in their efforts to successfully implement usability testing in their research, making usability testing inconsistent or ineffective in their studies. Moreover, many studies may fail due to the lack of usability in their processes.

03

Solution

The challenge that the UX4UX project tries to solve is the lack of use of usability testing in the medical research field. To achieve this, the project has created a web-based tool that helps the medical researcher to not only learn but also perform usability testing. Utilizing the User-Centered Design process, the project engaged in user studies and interviews aimed at learning the needs and challenges of the researchers before designing the platform that provides guidance and usability strategies for evaluation. This strategy not only helps the medical field but also improves the quality and success of any study that requires usability testing.
